Data Deletion Instructions — TLK Muse Connector

Last updated: 22 October 2026

TLK Muse Connector is a private app used by the administrator of the Facebook Page Tet Luu Kyaw - တက်လူကျော် စာပေ. The app does not keep a separate database of Facebook user data; content it processes (Page posts and comments) lives on Facebook under Meta's control.

How to request deletion

If content associated with you — for example a comment you made on the Page — appears through the Page and you want it removed:

  1. Email tetluukyaw@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include a link to the post or comment, or enough detail (date, text) for us to find it.
  3. We will remove the content using the Page's moderation tools and confirm by email. Requests are normally completed within 7 days.

You can also delete your own comments directly in Facebook at any time: open the comment, tap the options menu, and choose Delete.
