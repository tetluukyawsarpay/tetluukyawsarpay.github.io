Privacy Policy — TLK Muse Connector

Last updated: 22 October 2026

TLK Muse Connector is a private application used solely by the owner and administrator of the Facebook Page Tet Luu Kyaw - တက်လူကျော် စာပေ (a bookstore in Yangon, Myanmar) to manage that Page through the Facebook Graph API.

What the app does

The app is used to read the Page's own information and posts, read and reply to comments on the Page's posts, and publish posts and photos to the Page on the owner's instructions. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

Through Meta's API, the app accesses data belonging to the Page it is authorized for: Page profile information, Page posts, and comments and reactions on those posts. When a member of the public comments on the Page, that comment is processed only so the Page can read and respond to it.

Storage and retention

The app does not maintain a database of Facebook user data and does not store personal data on third-party servers. Access credentials are held in secure storage under the Page owner's control. API responses are processed only as needed to carry out the action the owner requested.

Sharing

No data obtained through the app is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is never used for advertising or profiling of individuals.

Your choices
